Before you ask.

What do you actually run?

The account, end to end. Hooks and angles, the edit, the posting rhythm, and the monthly report. The first week is setup; after that short-form goes out daily.

Is this organic, or is it ads?

Almost entirely organic, and the dashboards say so. Pivot Point Grips' 1.2M views came with 0.2% from ads, its two-week figure with 0.1%. One account on this page did run ads alongside: 29.2% of BKH's 5.4M reach was paid. We would rather write that down than have you find it later.

How long before anything moves?

Pivot Point Grips went from 116,498 views in the month before the takeover to 301,828 in the first fortnight after it, and 1,224,413 by the end of the first month. Houdini took two months to show up in orders. Two accounts, not a promise — but that is the shape of it.

Do you work with creators as well as brands?

Yes. For brands we run the account. For creators we manage the brand deals: who to take, what to charge, and getting paid.

What do you need from me to start?

Access to the account and whatever footage already exists. The first week is spent going through what has worked on the page before, so nothing gets rebuilt that was already working.

What does it cost?

It depends on the account, the cadence and whether creators are involved. The fifteen minutes ends with a number, not a proposal to be sent later.

Where are you, and who do you work with?

Miami, Florida, working with brands and creators across the United States. Calls run in Miami time.
